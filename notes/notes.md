## How to Use the Calculator

**Basic Operations**
Type numbers and operators directly or use the buttons.
Press `=` or hit `Enter` to calculate.

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `+` `-` `*` `/` | Add, Subtract, Multiply, Divide |
| `%` or `mod` | Modulo (remainder) |
| `xʸ` | Power — e.g. `math.pow(2,8)` |
| `√` | Square root — e.g. `math.sqrt(16)` |

**Math Functions**
- `sin`, `cos`, `tan` — trig functions (radians)
- `abs(x)` — absolute value
- `π` — inserts `math.pi`

**Keyboard Shortcuts**
- `Enter` → Calculate
- `Backspace` → Delete last character
- `Esc` → Clear all

**History**
Every calculation is saved to a local SQLite database.
Click **View calculation history** or switch to the History tab to review past results.
